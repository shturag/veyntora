# Salary bands 2026 (confidential, HR only)

Owner: HR. Readable only by the HR group.

Band S1 (junior specialist): 48,000 to 56,000 euros per year.
Band S2 (specialist): 56,000 to 68,000 euros per year.
Band S3 (senior specialist): 68,000 to 82,000 euros per year.

Salary reviews take place every April. Increases above 8 percent need the approval of the HR director.
