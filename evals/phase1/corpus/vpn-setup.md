# VPN setup for new laptops

Owner: IT Helpdesk. Last review: October 2026.

1. Install "Nordwind Connect" from the Software Center. Do not download VPN clients from the internet.
2. Sign in with your company account; multi-factor authentication is required.
3. Choose the gateway "vpn-eu-central" unless IT gave you a different one.

The VPN must be active for the HR portal and the finance tools. If the connection fails three times, open a ticket in the category "Network access". The helpdesk is reachable on extension 4400 from 07:00 to 19:00.
