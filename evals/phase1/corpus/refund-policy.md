# Refund policy

Version 3.2, valid from 1 September 2026. Owner: Customer Service.

## 4.1 Physical products

Customers can return physical products within 30 days of delivery. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. The refund goes to the original payment method within 5 working days after the return arrives at the warehouse in Kassel.

## 4.2 Digital products

Digital products (software licences, e-books, online courses) can be refunded within 14 days of purchase, but only if the download or streaming has not started. After access has started, the right of withdrawal expires.

## 4.3 Exceptions

Refunds above 500 euros need the approval of a Customer Service team lead. Goodwill refunds outside these rules are limited to 50 euros per customer and year.
