# Support service levels

Owner: Customer Service. Applies to all business customers.

| Priority | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 (system down) | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P2 (major function impaired) | 4 hours | 3 working days |
| P3 (minor issue) | 1 working day | 10 working days |

Tickets that miss the resolution target are escalated to the support lead automatically. Premium customers get P1 support around the clock; all other customers get support on working days from 08:00 to 18:00.
