# Onboarding checklist for team leads

Owner: People & Culture.

Before day one: order the laptop at least 10 working days ahead, request access to the team's SharePoint site, and assign an onboarding buddy.

In the first week: the new colleague completes the security training (about 45 minutes) and the data protection training. Book a feedback talk after 6 weeks and the probation review after 5 months.
